# MarketPulse TV — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-05-31_

MarketPulse TV ("the app") is a lean-back financial dashboard for Amazon Fire TV
and Roku. This policy describes exactly what data the app does and does not handle.

## Data We Collect

**None.** MarketPulse TV does not collect, store, or transmit any personal
information. Specifically:

- No account creation, login, or authentication.
- No name, email, address, or contact information.
- No payment or financial-account information (subscription billing, where
  offered, is handled entirely by the platform — Amazon Appstore or Roku — and
  the app never sees your payment details).
- No device identifiers, advertising IDs, or location data are collected by us.
- No analytics or tracking SDKs are embedded by us.

## How the App Works

The app displays publicly available market data (stock quotes, cryptocurrency
prices, and AI-summarized financial news) fetched from our backend API. These
requests contain no personal information — they ask only for the public market
data shown on screen.

## Third-Party Services

- **Our backend** (hosted on Vercel) proxies public market data from Alpaca
  Markets, CoinGecko, and a news provider, and generates news summaries via an
  AI model. It receives only the app's data requests, not personal data.
- **Roku** (free tier) may serve ads through the Roku Advertising Framework
  (RAF) and handles any subscription billing. Roku's own privacy policy governs
  that data; see https://docs.roku.com/published/userprivacypolicy.
- **Amazon** (Fire TV) handles subscription billing via Amazon In-App
  Purchasing. Amazon's privacy policy governs that data.

## Children's Privacy

The app is a general-audience financial information tool and is not directed at
children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new

## Long Description
```
MarketPulse TV turns your television into an always-on financial command center. Live stock prices, real-time cryptocurrency quotes, top market movers, AI-summarized news from Benzinga, the Fear & Greed sentiment gauge, and the upcoming earnings calendar — all on the big screen, designed for a lean-back, glance-and-go experience.

Eight curated pages with a premium glassmorphism "Midnight" design:
• HOME — top gainers/losers, Fear & Greed gauge, headlines, live ticker
• CRYPTO — top 20 coins with 24h change
• STOCKS — major U.S. equities, color-coded, selectable detail
• NEWS — Benzinga newswire with tickers and thumbnails
• CALENDAR — upcoming earnings with estimated EPS
• SENTIMENT — Fear & Greed index with context
• SETTINGS — data sources, refresh, theme, privacy
• UPGRADE — Free / Premium / Pro tiers

Live data via Alpaca Markets, CoinGecko, Benzinga, and Alternative.me. Auto-refreshes every 30 seconds with a scrolling chyron of the day's most-watched assets.
```
